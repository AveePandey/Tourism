Welcome to Nepal

Land of Himalayas, Ancient Cultures, and Breathtaking Adventures

Discover the Magic of Nepal

Nepal, nestled between India and Tibet, is a land of extraordinary natural beauty and cultural richness. From the towering peaks of the Himalayas to the lush jungles of the Terai lowlands, Nepal offers a diverse range of experiences for travelers. Explore ancient temples, trek through spectacular mountain landscapes, encounter rare wildlife, and immerse yourself in vibrant local traditions. Whether you seek adventure, spiritual enlightenment, or simply a chance to witness some of the world's most dramatic scenery, Nepal promises an unforgettable journey.

Top Destinations in Nepal

Kathmandu

Nepal's vibrant capital and cultural heart, featuring ancient temples, bustling markets, and stunning Durbar Square. Explore the UNESCO World Heritage sites including Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple) and Boudhanath Stupa.

Explore

Pokhara

A tranquil lakeside city with stunning views of the Annapurna range. Enjoy boating on Phewa Lake, paragliding adventures, and the gateway to the popular Annapurna Circuit trek.

Explore

Everest Base Camp

The legendary trekking destination that brings you face-to-face with the world's highest peak. Experience Sherpa culture, breathtaking mountain vistas, and the ultimate high-altitude adventure.

Explore

Chitwan National Park

Nepal's first national park and a UNESCO World Heritage site. Go on safari to spot endangered one-horned rhinoceros, Bengal tigers, and over 500 species of birds in this subtropical paradise.

Explore

Lumbini

The birthplace of Lord Buddha and a sacred pilgrimage site. Visit the Maya Devi Temple, explore peaceful monasteries built by different Buddhist communities from around the world.

Explore

Annapurna Circuit

One of the world's classic treks, circling the Annapurna massif. Experience dramatic changes in landscape, climate, and culture as you cross the challenging Thorong La Pass at 5,416m.

Explore

Bhaktapur

A medieval city known for its preserved ancient architecture and vibrant arts scene. Wander through the traffic-free streets to discover intricate woodcarvings, pottery squares, and traditional lifestyle.

Explore

Nagarkot

Famous for offering one of the best Himalayan panoramas, including views of Mount Everest on clear days. A perfect weekend getaway for stunning sunrise and sunset views over the mountains.

Explore

Upper Mustang

A remote, semi-autonomous region that was once the Kingdom of Lo. Explore the forbidden city of Lo Manthang with its unique Tibetan-influenced culture, mysterious caves, and stark desert landscapes.

Explore

Langtang Valley

An accessible trekking region with spectacular mountain views, diverse flora and fauna, and authentic Tamang culture. Experience glaciers, alpine meadows, and sacred lakes without the crowds.

About Nepal

Nepal, officially the Federal Democratic Republic of Nepal, is a landlocked country in South Asia. It is located mainly in the Himalayas, but also includes parts of the Indo-Gangetic Plain. With an estimated population of 30 million, Nepal is the 43rd most populous country in the world.

The mountainous north has eight of the world's ten tallest mountains, including the highest point on Earth, Mount Everest, called Sagarmatha in Nepali. It contains more than 240 peaks over 20,000 ft (6,096 m) above sea level. The southern Terai region is fertile and humid.

Nepal has a diverse geography, including fertile plains, subalpine forested hills, and eight of the world's ten tallest mountains, including Mount Everest, the highest point on Earth. Nepal is a multiethnic, multilingual, multi-religious and multicultural state, with Nepali as the official language.
